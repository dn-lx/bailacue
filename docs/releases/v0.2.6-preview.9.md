- **One Salsa and one Bachata Daily Pick:** the default daily mix chooses one available video for each selected dance style. Each style has its own cooldown fallback. A missing style is shown as an empty slot rather than replaced by a second video of another style.
- **Your rating leads:** eligible videos are ranked first by their 1–5 star rating. Labels, learning status, recency and More/Less feedback break ties within the same rating. A recently picked video rests when another eligible video of that style exists. If all of that style's eligible videos are recent, its highest-rated eligible video can repeat.
- **Minimum rating:** Settings lets you require 1★–5★ or higher. The picker never silently relaxes this requirement. A 4★ minimum can therefore leave the Bachata slot empty until a qualifying video is available.
- **Update today’s picks:** apply changed ratings immediately with this action on Today or Settings. Otherwise the day's valid choices stay stable. Changing the daily mix or minimum rating replans the selection; Skip today remains respected. Turning off One video per selected style restores an explicit total-count mode.
